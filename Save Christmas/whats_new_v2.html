Santa Must Save Xmas – Version 2.0

Santa is back on the ColecoVision! Fifteen years after the original 2011 release, the gift-catching chase gets a festive makeover. Here is what's new.

🎄 Brand-new title screen

  • A full-screen illustration of Santa's sleigh, his reindeer, a decorated tree and a snowy village under the moon.
  • Made within the real limits of the ColecoVision video chip (256×192 pixels, 2 colours per 8-pixel row) and shown in full on real hardware.

❄️ Smooth scrolling scenery

  • The Christmas trees, snowmen, stars and baubles now glide across the screen pixel by pixel instead of jumping 8 pixels at a time.
  • Stars and baubles float in the sky, while trees and snowmen stand on the snowy ground.
  • Scenery now slides in from the right edge of the screen instead of popping up halfway across.

🎵 Christmas music

  • A cheerful two-voice arrangement of Jingle Bells plays from the title screen through the whole game.
  • Sound effects play over the music, so you still hear every gift you catch.

🎮 Gameplay

  • Santa can now move up and down while firing. No more freezing in place while your shot is in flight!
  • The game runs at a steadier speed: the main loop was optimised and now almost always keeps up with the full 60 frames per second.

🎁 And also

  • Updated end screen: Merry Christmas 2011 and 2026!

Thanks for playing, and happy holidays to all ColecoVision fans!
